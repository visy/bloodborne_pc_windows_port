### Changed
- **Reflections (SSR): the reflection pass is shaded in pixel blocks at high resolutions** (Supermedo's bloodborne_pc 6085365d, GPL-2.0-or-later, ported with the pass found through the game profile). With `effect_ssr` on, a scene of 6 megapixels or more (native 4K) shades the reflection pass in 2x2 pixel blocks and one of 3 megapixels or more (1440p, 3440x1440) in 2x1 blocks, through VK_KHR_fragment_shading_rate; smaller scenes and GPUs without the extension shade every pixel as before, and nothing changes with SSR off. `BB_SSR_VRS=0` shades every pixel, `BB_SSR_SHADING_RATE=2x2` / `2x1` / `1x2` / `1x1` chooses the block. See docs/party/DEBUG_SWITCHES.md.
