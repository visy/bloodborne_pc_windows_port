### Fixed
- **Textures freed and re-made all the time on cards with more than 1.5 GB of video memory** (hitch082; since 0.8.1) the texture cache's 1.5 GiB cap was compared with all of the port's video memory instead of with the textures, so the clean-up ran at its emergency rule in nearly every frame: a texture not drawn for 8 frames was freed and uploaded again as soon as the camera turned back to it (40-140 textures a second while walking, hundreds within one frame at a doorway or a corner on every visit). The cap now counts the cached textures only; the cache still stops growing at about 1 GiB. `BB_GC_CAP_IMAGES=0` restores the old comparison.
