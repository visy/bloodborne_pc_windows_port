### Fixed
- **Fixed: the Noble Dress and other skirts no longer clip through the hunter above 30 FPS** (#37; Kyo / Jgamrot's yharnam-pc cloth work, Supermedo's Nightwatch cloth.py and deadinside28's `ClothStiffnessFactor` were tried against it, GPL-2.0-or-later). In the default Live FPS mode the cloth's constraint stiffness was never adapted to the frame rate (only the older frame-rate-hooks mode had that), so at 60 FPS and up a skirt's hem rode up into the body, worst at 120 and uncapped and sometimes staying that way until the next move. The cloth's stiffness now follows the square of the frame's length in 30 FPS frames; measured on the Noble Dress at 30, 60, 90, 120 and uncapped the hem hangs where it does at 30. `BB_CLOTH_FIX=0` restores the old behaviour. See docs/CLOTH_FIX.md.
