### Fixed
- **Short hitches when a new part of the map comes into view (a doorway, a corner)** (door082) the port copied a map piece's geometry to the video card at its first draw, so a new room coming into view put 45-200 MB of vertex data, the video memory for it and the write protection of its pages into one or two frames (30-40 ms on a fast, idle PC, 45-75 ms on a busy one), and on cards up to 8 GB again on a later visit. Geometry is now copied when the game creates it (mostly during the loading screen, 3 MB per frame while playing), before it is drawn, and stays on the card while the game keeps it and video memory is not short. Uses about 0.5 GB more video memory; nothing is copied ahead when the card is nearly full, and cards with less than 5 GB keep the old behaviour. `BB_BUF_PREUPLOAD=0` restores the old behaviour everywhere.
- **One 60-80 ms hitch per session at a random place** (door082) the first time the game drew a buffer lying across a 4 GiB boundary of its video memory, the port rebuilt its copy of that memory in the middle of the frame. This is now done during the first loading screen. `BB_ARENA_JOIN_AHEAD=0` restores the old behaviour.
