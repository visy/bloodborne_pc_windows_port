### Fixed
- **Enemies and Djura dying on their own in a party** (Nightwatch bug report): a death on one player's game that a non-player caused (an NPC's gatling fire, another enemy) was spread to everyone by the kill record, so in Old Yharnam dozens of enemies and Djura were put to death on the host and the other guests. Only deaths from a player's hit (or with no known attacker, like bleed or a fall) are shared now. `BB_PARTY_KILL_RECORD_NPC=0` restores the old behaviour.
- **First playthrough stuck in the prologue** (Nightwatch 783c7b9, vladtepes22): in a party, an awake Doll now counts as the prologue being over, so a new character that left the Dream without picking the starting weapons no longer stays in "first-dream" with no invasions or summons.
- **Direct-path test over a Nightwatch VPN** (Nightwatch ed6043c, vladtepes22): the test takes the public address from UPnP first and STUN only when UPnP gives none, and a test where nothing answered logs "direct no answer" instead of "direct 0 ms".
